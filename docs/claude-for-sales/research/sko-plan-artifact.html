The design

One account, tiered
Organizing principle: every rep picks one real account from their own territory at the door and carries it all day — Claude 101 → tiered breakout → 10-minute competition. Beginners produce a research brief; Enterprise produces a full account plan. Same account, different depth.

Why lean
A rep reuses what they can remember without opening a doc. Three chained skills = three failure points where a beginner needs zero.

The five beginner prompt cards
C1 · Company snapshot What they do, locations, segment, recent news.
C2 · Buying-committee mapping Redesigned Roles + publicly stated leadership from the company site, press, and trade-press quotes — confidence-tagged. Where sources only yield the role, output is "title to verify on LinkedIn" — LinkedIn is the rep's manual step, printed on the card. The skill never fabricates names.
C3 · Buying-signal scan Expansion, funding, new locations, leadership change, ops/IT job postings.
C4 · Incumbent-vendor check What workforce/ops tech they appear to use today — public signals only.
C5 · Contact-ready brief One screen to open a cold call: who to reach, the hook, one smart question.

Enterprise cards map to the 5 plan phases: setup → SF pull → research → synthesize → qualification translation. Five is a hard cap.

The Tuesday Card — tool-confusion firewall
Claude = "my thinking partner that researches and drafts."
Fourth Encyclopedia = "my phone lookup for product facts."
Fourth Brain = "coming later — Claude plugged into our internal docs."

Reps repeat these verbatim. Brain stays a 60-second teaser (Dave-driven fourth-playwright demo), never rep content.

Session structure

Setup out of the clock
Setup block · 15 min  At plenary-end, facilitator-led with an IT floater: device check → login + MFA → create Project → install skills → paste instructions → authenticate Salesforce (success = your opportunities appear). Anyone who fails a step is flagged to the floater and runs the breakout on the fallback pack — nobody debugs auth during exercise time.
Enterprise breakout — 60 min run-of-show

Dave floats all rooms during the 0:08–0:42 failure-dense window. Reinforcement: day-30 manager check · day-60 least-used-card refresher · day-90 competition re-run.

Competition

Fresh arena · judgeable
A
Competition Arena Project — blank (skills + starter instructions, no account context), created in a 5-min setup window. Timer starts at account reveal. No context bleed; setup never counts against the clock.
B
Answer-key packet — facilitators pre-research 2–3 candidate surprise accounts (via the EBR research stack, 1–2 days prior). Judges verify against the key, not the rep's own citations.
C
Quality gate — all sections present · confidence tags used · every named person traceable to the key or a clickable source (2 random claims spot-checked per finalist). Fastest valid brief wins = the certification moment.

Data handling

New · Codex #2
  • Own-territory rule — reps work only their own accounts; Salesforce sharing rules are the boundary.
  • No bulk contact exports in any card or skill; contact data stays in the rep's own Project.
  • Screen-share rule — facilitators project only the anonymized demo/fallback account, never a rep's live Project.
  • Competition accounts are public-web prospects or synthetic composites — never live pipeline.
  • Post-event — Projects live in the Fourth-managed workspace (org retention applies); "treat Project contents like Salesforce data." Runbook note reviewed by Sarah.
