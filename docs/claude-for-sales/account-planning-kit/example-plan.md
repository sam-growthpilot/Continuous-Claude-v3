## 8. Qualification Translation  — **THE QUALITY BAR**
*(Raw Salesforce fields → qualification / relationship-health language, MEDDPICC-style. This is the

**Do NOT dump fields. Translate them into buying-committee and qualification health.**

| Raw signal (SF / research) | Qualification translation (what it MEANS) |
|----------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| Opportunity Stage = "Discovery"; 1 contact on record; Activity count low/uncertain | **1 stakeholder engaged (the COO); economic buyer not yet identified. Single-threaded — the #1 risk.** (Not "activity count = low.") |
| Only COO publicly named; CFO/HR/IT roles unfilled in the map | **Committee mapped by role but under-identified: 1 of ~4 seats has a name. Multi-threading is the Q2 job.** |

| No labor-cost / turnover figures anywhere | **Metrics = UNQUANTIFIED. We have a pain thesis but no economic proof from the prospect yet — a discovery gap, not a value claim to assert.** |
| "HotSchedules or similar" on one posting | **Competition = SUSPECTED not confirmed. Decision Criteria unknown until incumbent + committee priorities surface.** |
| Opportunity Amount field populated | **Budget signal present in SF but not validated against an identified economic buyer — treat as unconfirmed until the CFO is met.** |
| No paper process / decision process in SF | **Decision Process = UNKNOWN. Paper Process not yet mapped. Both open items for Q2–Q3.** |

**One-paragraph qualification health (the read a manager wants):**
> Harbor & Vine is an early-stage, **single-threaded** new-logo opportunity in Discovery. **Three
> stakeholders' worth of committee is mapped by role, but only one — the COO — is engaged and named;
> the economic buyer (likely the CFO) is not yet identified.** We have a strong, sourced *Compelling
> Event* (the announced 10–12 unit expansion) but **metrics are unquantified** and the *incumbent is
> suspected, not confirmed*. The plan's near-term priority is exactly right: **multi-thread to the
> economic buyer and quantify the pain with the prospect's own numbers** before pushing on value.
> Health: promising trigger, thin qualification — de-risk by widening the committee, not by
> accelerating the pitch.

**Why this is the bar:** every line is *relationship-health / MEDDPICC language* ("economic buyer
not yet identified," "single-threaded," "Compelling Event candidate," "metrics unquantified") — NOT
a field dump ("activity count = 7," "stage = Discovery," "ownership = PE"). A rep's output passes the
competition only if their Qualification Translation reads like *this*, with every underlying claim
still confidence-tagged and every gap honestly flagged "verify manually."
