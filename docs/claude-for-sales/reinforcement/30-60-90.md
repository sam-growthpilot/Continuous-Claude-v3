# Post-SKO Reinforcement Plan — 30/60/90

**One page. Three checkpoints. One owner: the manager.**

---

## Why This Exists

Without reinforcement, reps lose **~87% of SKO content within 30 days**. Unreinforced training
converts to **5-7% ROI**; structured 30/60/90 reinforcement converts to **22-45% ROI**. A single
post-event survey ("did you use Claude?") will not move adoption — durable behavior change needs
a **minimum of 60-90 days** of scheduled touchpoints, not a one-time follow-up.
[Ascent Trainings — Post-Training Reinforcement](https://feeds.ascenttrainings.com/blog/post-training-reinforcement-program) ·
[Funnel Clarity — Sales Training Reinforcement](https://www.funnelclarity.com/sales-training/reinforcement)

Three checkpoints. Same owner every time: **the manager.** No new tooling, no extra headcount —
just three calendar holds that already exist (1:1, team session, live re-check).

---

## Day 30 — Habit Check

| | |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Manager, in the existing 1:1 |
| **Verifies** | Has the rep built **at least one real account plan or research brief in their Claude Project** — not the SKO practice account, a real territory account? |
| **Tier-specific artifact** | Starter/SDR: a 5-card research brief (C1–C5). Cockpit/Enterprise: an account-plan doc with at least Sections 1-4 drafted. |

**Facilitator script (manager, 1:1):**
> "Pull up your Claude Project — walk me through the account you've researched since SKO. Which
> card or section did you build, and what's still flagged 'not found in public sources — verify
> manually'?"

**Pass/fail signal:** rep can produce the Project and narrate one real card/section. If they
can't, that's the finding — not a lecture moment, a scheduling moment: block 20 minutes this week
to build one together.

---

## Day 60 — Refresher Micro-Session

| | |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Manager or team-level facilitator (not a full re-training — 15-20 min, team or 1:1) |
| **Verifies** | Rep can correctly rebuild the **least-used of the 5 cards** (per manager's Day-30 observations across the team) — plus a short knowledge check on confidence tags and the no-fabrication rule. |
| **Knowledge check (3 questions, oral or written)** | 1) What are the three confidence tags and when does each apply? 2) What's the exact phrase to use when a stakeholder name isn't found? 3) Is LinkedIn scraped automatically, or is it a manual step? |

**Facilitator script:**
> "Show me your last account brief — which card did you skip or rush? Let's rebuild it together
> right now, tagging every claim [VERIFIED] / [REPORTED] / [INFERRED] as we go."

**Pass/fail signal:** rep rebuilds the weak card unassisted after one walkthrough, and answers all
three knowledge-check questions correctly (LinkedIn = manual verification step is the one reps
most often get wrong — flag if missed).

---

## Day 90 — Graduation: Live Re-Check

| | |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Manager nominates; sales enablement/facilitator runs and judges |
| **Verifies** | Re-run the SKO competition format — fresh surprise account, 10-minute clock, same quality gate (all sections present, confidence tags used, every named person traceable to source). Fastest **valid** brief re-certifies. |

**Facilitator script:**
> "Same rules as SKO: fresh account revealed, 10-minute clock, judges spot-check two of your
> claims against the source. This is your recertification — show me the habit stuck."

**Pass/fail signal:** identical judging bar to the original competition. A rep who passes has
converted the SKO exercise into a durable, repeatable habit — that's graduation.

---

## Cadence Summary

| Day | Moment | Owner | Time cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | Habit check | Manager (1:1) | 5 min inside existing 1:1 |
| 60 | Refresher + knowledge check | Manager/facilitator | 15-20 min, team or 1:1 |
| 90 | Graduation re-check | Enablement/facilitator, manager nominates | 10-min timed brief per rep |

No new meetings required — each checkpoint rides inside a touchpoint that already exists on the
calendar. The only new discipline is asking the specific question above, every time.
