The certification moment.

Every rep, on a surprise account they've never seen, produces a valid research brief against a 10-minute clock. Passing the quality gate — not raw speed — is the certification. Fastest valid brief wins.

Judging order — fixed, non-negotiable
STEP 1
Minimum-quality veto
Any fabricated name, or an unknown not honestly flagged, disqualifies the brief — checked first, before speed matters.
→
STEP 2
Validity checklist
All sections present, confidence tags used, every name traceable (judges spot-check 2 random claims), sales vocabulary.
→
STEP 3
Speed tie-break
Only among valid briefs does submission time matter. A shallow-but-fast brief cannot win.

Judges score against a pre-built answer-key packet (2–3 candidate accounts, researched 1–2 days prior on the facilitator tools) — never against the rep's own citations. That's what makes "zero fabricated names" judgeable live. Surprise accounts are public-web prospects or synthetic composites — never a rep's live pipeline account.

COMPETITION SCORECARD — tap to test-drive

Step 1 · Minimum-quality veto — any fail disqualifies
⚠ Any veto fail → disqualified, regardless of speed.
Step 2 · Validity — all four must pass
◆ Certification: every rep who produces a valid brief is certified — the room's goal is to maximize certified reps, not crown one winner. The fastest valid brief is simply recognized.
