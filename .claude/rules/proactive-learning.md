## Summary (for continuity)

Store learnings at the moment of insight, not as post-processing. Triggers:
non-trivial bug fixes (3+ attempts or multi-file), architectural decisions,
codebase patterns, failed approaches, user corrections. After completing
debugging or multi-file changes, ask: "Is there something here worth
remembering for next time?"

Canonical skill covers: the full proactive-trigger table, scope detection
(`scope:project` vs `scope:global`), worked examples, and the store command.
